Everything else (carpet pattern, backlit onyx, wall panelling, the 3D skyline and its window atlas, felt layout, chips, slot symbols, sounds, music) is generated in code, or made for the game with ElevenLabs (`tools/audio/`), apart from the owner's own music logged above.
