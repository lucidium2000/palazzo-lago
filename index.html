How to play

Walk: WASD (Shift to hurry), mouse to look. Esc frees the cursor; drag to look and click the floor to walk there. M opens the map to jump to a game.

Sit down: walk up to a table or machine and press E. Walk away to stand up.

Bar & piano: click the bar to order (drinks are on the house), F to sip, Q to set it down. Tip the pianist at the jar on the piano.

Roulette: pick a chip on the table's panel (or keys 1–7), click the layout to bet. Click between numbers for splits, corners and streets. Space spins, Z undoes, C clears, R rebets.

Slots: walk up to a machine, use − / + to set your bet, press SPIN (or tap the screen). Info shows the paytable.

VR (Quest): left stick walks, right stick turns, point and pull the trigger (or pinch) to select or teleport. Grab with the grip button: chips from your rack (drop them on the felt to bet), the slot lever, glasses (raise one to your mouth to sip). Your left wrist has a menu.

Two players: on the start screen pick your character, then Host 2-player to get a room code (like LAGO-7K2F) and send it to a friend, who picks Join a friend and types it in. You'll see each other on the floor with name tags. Press T (or tap the room badge) to chat. Roulette and craps are shared: the host's table spins the wheel and rolls the dice, either of you can call the spin or shoot, and each of you bets your own chips. Big wins are announced to your friend.

Play money only. Chips have no cash value. 18+.
