How to play

Walk: WASD (Shift to hurry), mouse to look. Esc frees the cursor; drag to look and click the floor to walk there. M opens the map to jump to a game.

Sit down: walk up to a table or machine and press E. Walk away to stand up.

Bar & piano: click the bar to order (drinks are on the house), F to sip, Q to set it down, X to throw it (glasses break!). Drinks left on tables can be picked up too, and so can almost anything small: chips, souvenir dice, bottles (F to swig, hold P to pour, they smash), limes, napkins, menus, ashtrays, champagne buckets. Click to pick up, X to throw, Q to set it down; thrown things bounce, roll, knock other things over and slide off tables. Tip the pianist at the jar on the piano.

Roulette: pick a chip on the table's panel (or keys 1–7), click the layout to bet. Click between numbers for splits, corners and streets. Space spins, Z undoes, C clears, R rebets.

Slots: walk up to a machine, use − / + to set your bet, press SPIN (or tap the screen). Info shows the paytable.

VR (Quest): left stick walks, right stick turns, point and pull the trigger (or pinch) to select or teleport. Grab with the grip button: chips from your rack (drop them on the felt to bet), the slot lever, glasses (raise one to your mouth to sip; swing and let go to throw it; tip it over to pour it on anything, or anyone; knock glasses together hard and they smash), and loose things lying around: chips, dice, bottles (swig, pour, smash), fruit, napkins, menus, ashtrays, champagne buckets. Sweep your hand across a table and you knock things flying. In a two-player game, squeeze into a fist and throw real punches at your friend: hits to the head or body land, their hand in the way blocks, and run out of stamina and you go down. Press Y on your left controller to open the full menu on your left wrist (with hand tracking it's always there): Go (jump to any game), Room (host or join a two-player game with a code keypad, change your character, voice chat, quick chat) and Menu (music, sound, help, reset chips, exit VR).

Two players: on the start screen pick your character, then Host 2-player to get a room code (like LAGO-7K2F) and send it to a friend, who picks Join a friend and types it in. You'll see each other on the floor with name tags. Press T (or tap the room badge) to chat, and Voice to talk (V mutes); in VR it's under Room on your wrist, and your friend's voice comes from their avatar. Roulette and craps are shared: the host's table spins the wheel and rolls the dice, either of you can call the spin or shoot, and each of you bets your own chips. Big wins are announced to your friend.

Hanging out: in VR you see your own body and hands. With your friend: slap open hands up high for a high five, squeeze both grips and bump fists for a fist bump, or hold hands together at waist height to shake hands. On a screen, open chat (T) and use the High five / Fist bump / Shake hands / Wave buttons; your avatar holds out its hand, and if your friend does the same (or meets it in VR), it lands.

Play money only. Chips have no cash value. 18+.
