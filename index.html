How to play

Walk: WASD or the arrow keys (Shift to hurry, Space to jump, hold C to crouch, or to dive in the pool), mouse to look. Esc frees the cursor; drag to look and click the floor to walk there. M opens the map to jump to a game.

Sit down: walk up to a table or machine and press E. Walk away to stand up.

Bar & piano: click the bar to order (drinks are on the house), F to sip, Q to set it down, X to throw it (glasses break!). Drinks left on tables can be picked up too, and so can almost anything small: chips, souvenir dice, bottles (F to swig, hold P to pour, they smash), limes, napkins, menus, ashtrays, champagne buckets. Click to pick up, X to throw, Q to set it down; thrown things bounce, roll, knock other things over and slide off tables. Tip the pianist at the jar on the piano. G clinks glasses with someone close by. The pool bar serves drinks too (frozen ones, beer, cocktails): click its counter.

The piano: walk up and press E to sit at the keys (in VR, touch or step onto the bench and play with your fingertips; A or X is the sustain pedal). On a screen the keyboard plays it: A S D F G H J K L ; ' the white keys, W E T Y U O P the black ones, hold Shift to play louder, Z / X an octave down / up, Space the pedal, Esc to stand. Or click the keys.

Pool party: take the lifts (or the corridor) out to the pool deck: the DJ is playing and the floor in front of the booth lights up with the beat. In VR just bob your head to the beat and your body dances with you; throw both hands up when the drop lands for confetti and a cheer. On a screen press J to dance (again for the next move: groove, fist pump, step and clap, spin, hands up). Your friends see you dance. Climb up behind the DJ's decks and scratch: in VR lay a hand on a record and move it, on a screen click and drag it. In the pool, hold C (VR: click and hold the right stick in, or push your hands up through the water) to dive. The stage mic: walk up to the mic on the pool stage (behind it, where the singer stands) and your voice comes out of the pool's speakers, with a touch of reverb and echo, and the music dips while you talk; your friends hear you on the PA too. The first time, allow the browser to use your microphone. In VR you can lift the mic off its stand between Orlo's sets and hold it to your mouth. While Orlo's on you're singing along over his track. Stage mic monitor (below, or the wrist menu) turns off hearing yourself.

The Promenade: food from the counters: F takes a bite (VR: hold it to your mouth). At the Lago Luxe boutique pick up sunglasses or a hat, pay at the counter, then F puts it on (VR: hold shades to your eyes, a hat on your head) and U takes it off. In the arcade walk up to a cabinet (VR: grab its stick, poke its buttons): arrows / WASD are the stick, Space, X and C the buttons, Esc to step away; each game shows its keys along the bottom. G tosses a claw prize you're holding.

Fights: push someone too far and they square up. On a screen click (or F) to punch and hold H or the right mouse button to block; in VR make fists and swing, and get your hands in the way (fists up by your face) to block. With a friend, B throws a jab.

Your chips: every game starts you with $2,000. In VR, turn your left palm up and your chip tray pops up over it: point at a stack to bet with that chip, or grab one (hold to scoop more) and set it down on a spot. Then point (index finger out, other fingers curled; on a controller squeeze the grip, finger off the trigger) and press a betting spot: your chip rides on your fingertip and drops where you press. An open hand on the felt never bets. Drop chips back on the tray, your wrist or your rail to put them away; tap your loose chips with an open hand to scoop them up. The wallet on your watch (Y) has the same chips and the table's buttons. Chips you find lying around count too: set one on a betting spot and it plays.

Roulette: pick a chip along the bottom of the screen (or keys 1–7), click the layout to bet. Click between numbers for splits, corners and streets. No need to spin: when your bets are down and you stop placing chips for a few seconds (or about 20 seconds after the first bet), the croupier calls “No more bets” and spins. Space (or READY) tells him you're done. Z undoes, C clears, R rebets. In VR, turn your left palm up for your chip tray: take chips with your right hand (one per grab, hold for more) or point at a stack to pick it, then point and press the layout to place chip after chip.

Poker room: through the gilded doorway in the west wall (Map → Poker Room). No-limit Texas Hold'em, blinds $5/$10: sit in a free chair (a regular gives up theirs if you need it) and you buy in for $1,000 from your chips; stand up to cash out. On a screen: Fold, Check/Call, the raise slider and Raise/Bet, All in; your cards stand up facing you. In VR: cup a hand over your cards (or pick them up) to look; knock the felt twice to check; sweep your cards toward the dealer to fold; take chips from the rack at your right (hold your hand there to scoop more) and let go of them over the line in front of you to call or raise, or pick up your whole stack and push it out to go all in. Your palm-up chip tray works here too and deals from your stack: grab chips and let go over your line, or point at a stack and press the line to push out chip after chip. With friends, the host deals; nobody sees anyone else's cards until the showdown.

Suite 3201 & the lifts: touch the speaker on the bedside table to play music in your room (the lounge set, Orlo Vante's songs, the pool DJ's set; touch again for the next, round to off); the lifts play soft lounge music as you ride. At the minibar, tip bottles over the empty glasses to pour and mix: make one of the house recipes on the card and the room calls it. Walk onto the bed, a pool lounger or a cabana's daybed to sit on it.

Slots: walk up to a machine, use − / + to set your bet, press SPIN (or tap the screen). Info shows the paytable.

Photos: pick up a camera (on the bar and at the pool bar, or "Get a camera" in the wrist menu), frame the shot on its screen and pull the trigger. Poke the little gold button for a selfie. Your Polaroids go up on the board by the bar; on a screen, K takes a photo and the Photos button lets you download them.

VR, hands first: you do everything with your hands. Touch buttons and menu panels with your index finger to press them, grab things with the grip. Laser pointers are off by default (turn them on, pick snap or smooth turning, turn speed and the comfort vignette in the wrist menu, Menu tab). The first time a gesture applies, a small hint shows by your hand for a few seconds, once; Show hints and Reset hints are in the Menu tab too.

VR (Quest): left stick walks, right stick turns (click it in and hold to crouch, or to swim down in the pool), point and pull the trigger (or pinch) to select or teleport. Grab with the grip button: chips from your rack (drop them on the felt to bet), the slot lever, glasses (raise one to your mouth to sip; swing and let go to throw it; tip it over to pour it on anything, or anyone; knock glasses together hard and they smash), and loose things lying around: chips, dice, bottles (swig, pour, smash), fruit, napkins, menus, ashtrays, champagne buckets. Sweep your hand across a table and you knock things flying. In a two-player game, squeeze into a fist and throw real punches at your friend: hits to the head or body land, their hand in the way blocks, and run out of stamina and you go down. Press Y on your left controller to open the full menu on your left wrist (with hand tracking it's always there): Go (jump to any game), Room (host or join a room with a code keypad, see where your friends are and jump to them, tonight's scoreboard, change your character, voice chat, quick chat) and Menu (music, sound, help, reset chips, exit VR).

With friends (up to 4): on the start screen pick your character, then Host a room to get a room code (like LAGO-7K2F) and send it to up to three friends, who pick Join a friend and type it in. You'll see each other on the floor with name tags in your own colours (they stay readable across the room and show faintly through walls). The scoreboard under the room badge shows everyone's chips won or lost tonight and where each friend is; Go takes you straight to them. Press T (or tap the room badge) to chat, and Voice to talk (V mutes); in VR it's under Room on your wrist, and your friend's voice comes from their avatar. Roulette and craps are shared: the host's table spins the wheel and rolls the dice, the host's croupier calls the spin for everyone once the bets are in, and anyone can shoot the dice, and each of you bets your own chips. Big wins are announced to your friend.

Hanging out: in VR you see your own body and hands. With your friend: slap open hands up high for a high five, squeeze both grips and bump fists for a fist bump, or hold hands together at waist height to shake hands (gripping? give it a shake). To dab together, grab each other's hand (both grips), hold it still a moment, then pull apart still gripping. On a screen, open chat (T) and use the High five / Fist bump / Shake hands / Wave buttons; your avatar holds out its hand, and if your friend does the same (or meets it in VR), it lands. Press G (or Dab in chat) next to a friend and you both dab.
